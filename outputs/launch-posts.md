## Post 2 — The product reveal (honest, anti-inflater positioning)

**Platform:** X thread + LinkedIn
**Media:** screenshot of a match report (score + matched/missing keywords)

> Yesterday's post was the problem. Today, the thing I built.
>
> It's a pre-application fit check: upload your resume, paste the job description, get a match score + missing skills + risk flags.
>
> But the score is not the point.
>
> The point is tracking every application and seeing what actually works:
>
> - One master resume
> - Every job scored against it
> - Log interviews / rejections / offers
> - See which roles actually lead somewhere
>
> It also tells you when something is a *capability* gap, not a wording gap. It will never tell you to stuff keywords into a resume you don't back up.
>
> Because we want to see what you actually did — not an AI-polished list of everything.
>
> Link in reply 👇

## Post 3 — The shareable-result hook (turns users into the channel)

**Platform:** X / LinkedIn / TikTok-Reels script
**Media:** the "stat card" image (Applied · Interviews · Offers)

> Applied to 40 roles. No interviews. Sound familiar?
>
> Most of the time it's not your skills — it's that your resume never contains the exact evidence the job description is searching for.
>
> Here's the 2-minute fix:
>
> 1. Take your master resume.
> 2. Score it against the JD you're about to apply to.
> 3. Look at the "not found in resume" list — not to stuff words in, but to find what you did and forgot to write down.
> 4. Track the outcome. Watch your interview rate climb.
>
> Post your score below and I'll tell you what the biggest gap is.

**TikTok/Reels script (30–45s):**
> "Why am I not getting interviews? I applied to 40 jobs…" → show the stat card → paste a JD → show the match score drop → show missing keywords → "I fixed two bullets and it changed the outcome."

**Hashtags:** `#jobsearch #layoffs #careeradvice #interview`

## Posting notes

- **CTAs:** always link to the live app; on X put the link in the first reply (algorithm favors no-link posts).
- **Engagement hook:** end posts 1–3 with a question ("What's the #1 thing you check before applying?") to get replies.
